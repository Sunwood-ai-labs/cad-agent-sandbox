# Botanical CAD Comparison Theme

## Style Prompt

Simple botanical benchmark film: a calm ivory canvas with moss green typography, clay accents, and subtle leaf-line ornamentation. The tone is clear, practical, and handmade rather than glossy. CAD screenshots and orthographic views are treated like specimen plates in a workshop notebook.

## Colors

- Background: `#F4F1E7`
- Ink: `#243629`
- Moss: `#597B54`
- Sage: `#9BAE84`
- Clay accent: `#B96F4C`
- Charcoal data: `#2E3134`

## Typography

- Display: `Fraunces`, with `Noto Sans JP` fallback for Japanese labels.
- Data and badges: `IBM Plex Mono`, with `Consolas` fallback.
- Body: `Noto Sans JP`, `Segoe UI`, sans-serif.

## Motion

- Calm blur crossfades between scenes.
- Elements enter with small vertical, scale, or x-axis offsets.
- Leaf ornamentation drifts slowly and never competes with CAD images.

## What NOT to Do

- No neon, cyber, or dark dashboard styling.
- No crowded spec tables that require pausing to read.
- No red error styling for tool limitations; use clay callouts.
- No decorative cards inside cards.
