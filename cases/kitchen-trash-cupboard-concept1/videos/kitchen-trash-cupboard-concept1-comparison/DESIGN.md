# Concept 1 Kitchen Cupboard Verification Film

## Style Prompt

Calm botanical CAD verification film for a family kitchen storage concept. Use an ivory canvas, maple-paper warmth, moss green structure, and clay callouts. The concept sheet and generated CAD images should feel like evidence pinned into a tidy design review board, not a glossy product ad.

## Colors

- Background: `#F4F1E7`
- Paper: `#FFF9EA`
- Ink: `#243629`
- Moss: `#597B54`
- Sage: `#9BAE84`
- Clay: `#B96F4C`
- Data charcoal: `#2E3134`

## Typography

- Display: `Fraunces`, with `Noto Sans JP` fallback for Japanese.
- Data: `IBM Plex Mono`, with `Consolas` fallback.
- Body: `Noto Sans JP`, `Segoe UI`, sans-serif.

## Motion

- Gentle blur crossfades connect scenes.
- Evidence images enter with slight vertical, scale, or x-axis offsets.
- No jump cuts. The concept sheet remains a recurring visual anchor.

## What NOT to Do

- Do not show stale perfect-score claims.
- Do not hide the strict scoring penalties.
- Do not treat generated CAD as a manufacturing sign-off.
- Do not omit the attached concept sheet image.
